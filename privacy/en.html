This Privacy Policy explains how Drawing Board (the "App") handles information.

1. What we do not collect

The App does not collect, upload or share any personal identifying information. There is no account system, and we never ask for your name, phone number, email address or location.

2. Content you create

  1. 1. Your colouring pages, free drawings and photo doodles are stored only on this device, in the App's private app sandbox.
  2. 2. They never leave your device unless you export them yourself through the system share sheet.
  3. 3. Uninstalling the App deletes them all, so please back up anything you want to keep.

3. Photo permission

  1. 1. When you use Photo Doodle, the App asks for access to your photo library or camera so you can pick an image to draw on.
  2. 2. The permission is requested only when you tap it, and is used only for drawing locally. We do not read, upload or analyse the contents of your photos.
  3. 3. You can turn the permission off at any time in your device Settings.

4. Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple App Store. We never see or store your payment details. The App only receives the purchase result from Apple in order to unlock VIP features.

5. Third-party components

The App has no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and makes no network requests for your content. The open-source line art bundled with the App is licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0; the full credits are available inside the App.

6. Your rights

If you have any question about this policy, contact us using the contact details on the App Store listing. We reply within 15 business days.

7. Children

Drawing Board is a drawing tool for all ages, and children may use it with a parent or guardian. We follow data-minimisation: we collect no personal information from anyone, adult or child. A guardian can remove all local data by uninstalling the App.

8. Policy updates

If this policy changes, we will let you know clearly inside the App.
